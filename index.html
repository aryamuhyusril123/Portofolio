About Me

My Story

I am Arya Muhamad Yusril, an Informatics student who sees the world as a blend of logical structure and visual beauty.

Through my lens, I capture narratives that cannot be explained by lines of code—from the hustle and bustle of the streets to the tranquility of natural landscapes.

As a first-semester Informatics student, I combine visual storytelling with technology, creating images that speak louder than words.
